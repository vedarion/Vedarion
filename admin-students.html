Drill access

The checkbox authorizes a student to open and create drills containing LSAC content. The date is optional: leave it blank for open-ended access, or set it to the end of their package and access shuts off automatically the day after. Turning access off never deletes anything — their history stays, and re-checking the box restores everything instantly.
