Phenomenon & Hypothesis

Two terms the LSAT uses in Logical Reasoning and Reading Comprehension answer choices.

Memorize these
PHENOMENON
Something that needs to be explained.
Plural: phenomena
HYPOTHESIS
Someone's explanation for that phenomenon.
Their answer to why it's happening.
In a stimulus
Robins usually sing during the day. However, in the town of Brookdale, robins have started singing in the middle of the night. Ornithologists believe that the newly installed bright streetlights in Brookdale cause the robins to sing at night.
Phenomenon
Robins have started singing in the middle of the night.
Hypothesis
The new streetlights are causing it.
How the LSAT tests it
A hypothesis is just one possible explanation. If another explanation fits the phenomenon just as well, the hypothesis is weaker.
ANOTHER EXPLANATION (WEAKENS)Brookdale is very noisy during the day, so night is the only time the robins can be heard. Same phenomenon, no streetlights needed.
Always ask: Is there another way to explain the phenomenon?
