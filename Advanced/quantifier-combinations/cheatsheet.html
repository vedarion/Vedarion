Quantifier Combinations

Translate first, then combine.

To combine correctly, translate each sentence correctly first.
What combines
CombinationConclusion
All + AllAll
Most + AllMost
Some + AllSome
Most + Most(same group)Some
In a chain, the smaller piece comes before the all. The conclusion keeps the first word.
Same group vs. chain
SAME GROUP
Both start from Students.
Most students are athletes. Most students get free lunch.
So some athletes get free lunch.
CHAIN
The second starts from Athletes.
Most students are athletes. Most athletes get free lunch.
No conclusion.
When you see most and most, check whether both sentences start with the same group.
What doesn't combine
All + SomeNo conclusion
All + MostNo conclusion
Some + SomeNo conclusion
Some + MostNo conclusion
Most + SomeNo conclusion
Most + Most(chain)
No conclusion
If an answer choice concludes something from a combination that doesn't work, it's wrong, even if it could be true.
