Strengthen & Weaken

Flowchart Worksheet
STEP 1
Read the Question Stem
  • Identify whether it's Strengthen or Weaken
↓
STEP 2
Break Down the Stimulus
  • Identify conclusion and support as you read
↓
STEP 3
Core Argument
  • Simplify into premise → conclusion in plain form
↓
STEP 4
Analysis
  • Predict: Why does the conclusion fall short of the support?
↓
STEP 5
Answer Choices (Loop)
  • 1. Relevance Test: Does it connect to the premise in any way? (low bar) If yes, how?
  • 2. Impact Test: Premise + Answer = What impact on conclusion? Keep if any impact in the right direction.
  • Tip: If AC is tough, shallow dip → ask if it's on-topic and in the right direction before deep analysis
↓
Final Selection
Pick the one with fewest linguistic issues. No unreasonable assumptions between words and reasoning.
