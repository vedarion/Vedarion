Strengthen & Weaken

STEP 1
Read the Question Stem
  • Identify whether it's Strengthen or Weaken
↓
STEP 2
Break Down the Stimulus
  • Identify conclusion and support as you read
↓
STEP 3
Core Argument
  • Simplify into premise → conclusion in plain form
↓
STEP 4
Analysis
  • Predict: Why does the conclusion fall short of the support?
↓
STEP 5
Answer Choices (Loop)
  • Relevance Test: Does it connect to premise/conclusion? If it ignores or negates a premise → discard
  • Effect Test: If true, does it push the conclusion? (↑ Strengthen / ↓ Weaken)
  • Extra Tip: If AC is tough, shallow dip → ask if it's on-topic and in the right direction before deep analysis
  • Selection: Pick the strongest relevant effect
↓
Final Choice
Confirm the selected answer is both relevant and produces the required effect
