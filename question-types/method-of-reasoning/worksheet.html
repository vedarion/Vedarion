METHOD OF REASONING

Work through any Method of Reasoning question using the strategy.

Lesson Worksheet Cheat Sheet
Question
1
Identify the Question Stem
  • "...method of reasoning used in the argument?"
  • "The argument proceeds by..."
  • "The argument derives its conclusion by..."
  • "...technique or strategy employed by the argument?"
↓
2
Read the Stimulus

Read the full stimulus carefully before you label anything.

↓
3
Label the Premises and Conclusion

Go sentence by sentence and choose each one's role.

↓
4
Note How the Premises Support the Conclusion

What is the author doing with the premises to reach the conclusion? Describe it before looking at the answer choices.

Supporting their conclusion
  • Applying a rule or principle
  • Providing an example
  • Appealing to an analogy
  • Weighing options
Responding to another argument
  • Disputing a premise
  • Questioning an assumption
  • Providing counterevidence
  • Offering an alternative explanation
  • Drawing a distinction
  • Pointing out a flaw in reasoning
↓
5
Check Each Answer Choice

Is every word accurately describing how the premises support the conclusion? Start with the choices that match your prediction.
